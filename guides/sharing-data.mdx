## Overview

Health Manager allows you to share your health data with other users in read-only mode. This is useful for:

- Sharing with family members who help manage your health
- Providing access to caregivers
- Allowing healthcare providers to review your records
- Enabling remote health monitoring by trusted individuals

<Info>
  All shared access is read-only. Other users can view your data but cannot modify or delete your records.
</Info>

## Before You Start

To share data with someone, they must:

1. Have a registered account in your Health Manager system
2. Have set up their username (Nick) in their profile

<Tip>
  Ask the person you want to share with for their exact username before starting the sharing process.
</Tip>

## How to Share Your Data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Share Data">
    Go to your Profile section and select "Share Data" (Compartir Datos).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for the User">
    Start typing the username of the person you want to share with. The system will show matching users as you type.
    
    <Note>
      You need to type at least 2 characters before search results appear.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the User">
    When you see the correct user in the search results, click on their username to select them.
    
    The search will show up to 5 matching users at a time. If you don't see the person you're looking for, continue typing to narrow the results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant Access">
    Click the "Add" or "Grant Access" button to share your data with the selected user.
    
    <Info>
      The user will immediately gain read-only access to all your health records.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirmation">
    You'll see a confirmation message indicating that access has been granted successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Username Search

The username search system is designed to help you find users quickly:

- **Partial Matching**: Search works with partial usernames (e.g., "john" will find "john_doe")
- **Real-time Results**: Results update as you type
- **Excludes You**: You won't see your own username in search results
- **Limited Results**: Shows top 5 matches to keep the interface clean

## Managing Shared Access

### Viewing Who Has Access

In the Share Data section, you can see a list of all users who currently have access to your health records:

- Username of each viewer
- User's full name
- Options to revoke access

### Revoking Access

If you need to remove someone's access:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share Data Settings">
    Navigate to Profile > Share Data
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the User">
    Locate the user in your list of current viewers
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remove Access">
    Click the remove or revoke button next to their name
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    The user will immediately lose access to your health data
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Access removal is immediate. The user will no longer be able to view your health records after you revoke their permission.
</Note>

## What Shared Users Can See

When you grant someone access, they can view:

- All your medical appointments
- Exercise activity logs
- Weight measurements and charts
- Heart rate measurements
- Medical documents and files
- Calendar view of your health activities

## What Shared Users Cannot Do

<Info>
  Shared access is strictly read-only. Users with access cannot:
</Info>

- Add new records to your account
- Edit existing records
- Delete any of your data
- Change your profile settings
- Share your data with additional users
- Export your data (only you can export your own data)

## Validation and Safety Features

Health Manager includes several safety features to protect your data:

### Automatic Validations

- **Username Verification**: The system verifies that the username exists before allowing you to grant access
- **Self-Sharing Prevention**: You cannot share data with yourself
- **Duplicate Prevention**: You cannot grant access to the same user twice

### Error Messages

You'll receive clear error messages if:

- The username doesn't exist: "We couldn't find any user with that username"
- You try to share with yourself: "You cannot share data with yourself"
- The user already has access: "This user already has access to your data"

<Tip>
  If you receive an error about a username not existing, double-check the spelling with the person you're trying to share with.
</Tip>

## Privacy Best Practices

### Only Share When Necessary

- Grant access only to people who genuinely need to see your health information
- Review your list of viewers periodically
- Remove access when it's no longer needed

### Communicate Clearly

- Inform users when you grant them access
- Explain what they can and cannot do
- Let them know if you revoke their access

### Regular Audits

- Periodically review who has access to your data
- Remove access for users who no longer need it
- Update your sharing settings when your care team changes

<Note>
  As an administrator, you should also educate users about the importance of keeping their usernames private and only sharing with trusted individuals.
</Note>

## Accessing Shared Data

If someone has shared their data with you:

1. Navigate to the shared users section in the application
2. Select the user whose data you want to view
3. Browse their health records in read-only mode
4. Return to your own data by switching back to your profile

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I share with users outside my Health Manager installation?**

A: No, you can only share with users registered in the same Health Manager system. Each installation maintains its own separate user database.

**Q: Will shared users receive notifications?**

A: Check your system's notification settings. Notification behavior may vary based on your configuration.

**Q: Can I limit what specific data is shared?**

A: Currently, sharing is all-or-nothing. When you grant access, the user can view all your health records.
