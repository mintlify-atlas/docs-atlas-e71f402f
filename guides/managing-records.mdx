## Overview

Health Manager allows you to track various types of health information including medical appointments, exercise activities, weight measurements, and heart rate data. All records are displayed in an interactive calendar interface.

## Types of Health Records

Health Manager supports four main types of health records:

1. **Medical Appointments**: Track doctor visits, consultations, and medical procedures
2. **Exercise Activities**: Log workouts and physical activities
3. **Weight Measurements**: Monitor weight changes over time
4. **Heart Rate Measurements**: Track heart rate readings

## Adding a New Record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Calendar">
    Navigate to the main calendar view. This is typically your home screen after logging in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Add Button">
    Look for the `+` button in the lower right corner of the screen.
    
    <Info>
      The floating action button is your quick access point for creating any type of health record.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Record Type">
    Choose the type of record you want to add:
    - Medical Appointment
    - Exercise Activity
    - Weight Measurement
    - Heart Rate Measurement
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Details">
    Enter the relevant information for your record. Each type has specific fields:
    
    **Medical Appointments:**
    - Date and time
    - Doctor or specialist name
    - Purpose of visit
    - Notes or follow-up actions
    
    **Exercise Activities:**
    - Date and duration
    - Type of exercise
    - Intensity level
    - Additional notes
    
    **Weight Measurements:**
    - Date
    - Weight value
    - Optional notes
    
    **Heart Rate Measurements:**
    - Date and time
    - Heart rate value (BPM)
    - Context (resting, active, etc.)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the Record">
    Click the save button to add the record to your health history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Your Records

### Calendar View

The calendar provides a visual overview of all your health activities:

- Records appear on their corresponding dates
- Different record types are displayed with distinct visual indicators
- Click on any day to view detailed information

<Tip>
  The calendar view helps you identify patterns in your health data at a glance.
</Tip>

### Viewing Day Details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click on a Date">
    Click any day in the calendar that contains records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Record Details">
    A detailed view will open showing all records for that date, organized by type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracking Weight Over Time

Weight measurements include special visualization features:

- **Evolution Charts**: View your weight changes over time with interactive graphs
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify patterns in your weight management
- **Historical Data**: Access all past measurements in chronological order

<Note>
  Regular weight logging helps you and your healthcare providers track progress and make informed decisions.
</Note>

## Editing and Deleting Records

You can modify or remove records as needed:

1. Navigate to the record you want to edit
2. Click on the record to open its details
3. Use the edit button to modify information
4. Use the delete button to remove the record permanently

<Info>
  Deleted records cannot be recovered, so always confirm before removing important health data.
</Info>

## Best Practices

### Regular Updates

- Log health data consistently for accurate tracking
- Add notes to provide context for measurements
- Record appointments immediately after scheduling

### Organization

- Use clear, descriptive titles for medical appointments
- Include relevant details in notes fields
- Keep exercise logs detailed for better insights

### Data Accuracy

- Enter measurements as soon as possible after taking them
- Double-check values before saving
- Include time of day for context-sensitive measurements

<Tip>
  Setting a daily reminder to log your health data can help build a consistent tracking habit.
</Tip>

## Medical History Storage

In addition to structured records, Health Manager provides digital storage for medical documents:

- Upload reports and test results
- Store prescriptions and treatment plans
- Keep insurance documents organized
- Access files anytime from your profile
