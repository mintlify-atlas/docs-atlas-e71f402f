## Overview

Health Manager allows you to export your complete health history as a PDF document. This is useful for:

- Bringing a printed summary to doctor appointments
- Keeping offline backups of your health records
- Sharing data with healthcare providers outside the system
- Maintaining personal archives
- Insurance documentation

## What Gets Exported

When you generate a PDF export, the following data is included:

- **Medical Appointments**: All scheduled and completed appointments with details
- **Exercise Activities**: Complete log of physical activities and workouts
- **Weight Measurements**: All recorded weight data points
- **Heart Rate Measurements**: All heart rate readings

<Info>
  The export includes all your historical data, sorted by date in descending order (most recent first).
</Info>

## How to Export Your Data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Export">
    Go to your Profile section or Settings area where the export option is available.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Download History">
    Click on the "Download History" or "Export Data" button.
    
    <Note>
      The export process may take a few moments depending on the amount of data in your account.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for Generation">
    The system will compile all your health records into a comprehensive PDF report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    Your browser will automatically download the PDF file with a filename format:
    
    `historial-salud-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`
    
    Where the date represents when the export was generated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Your Export

### File Information

Each exported PDF includes:

- **Your Name**: As registered in your profile
- **Export Date**: The date when the report was generated
- **Complete Record Set**: All health records from your account

### Data Organization

Records in the PDF are organized:

- **Chronologically**: Sorted by date with most recent entries first
- **By Type**: Different record types are clearly distinguished
- **With Details**: All relevant information for each record is included

<Tip>
  The most recent data appears first in the export, making it easy to review your current health status quickly.
</Tip>

## Export Features

### Automatic Sorting

The export automatically combines all record types and sorts them by date, giving you a comprehensive timeline of your health activities.

### Complete Data Inclusion

Every piece of information you've entered is included:

- Dates and times
- Measurements and values
- Notes and descriptions
- All custom fields you've filled out

### Professional Formatting

The PDF is formatted for professional use:

- Clean, readable layout
- Clear section headers
- Organized data presentation
- Suitable for medical settings

## Use Cases

### Doctor Appointments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export Before Appointment">
    Generate your PDF a day or two before your scheduled appointment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Data">
    Check the export to ensure all relevant information is included.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Print or Share">
    Print the PDF or email it to your healthcare provider's office.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring to Appointment">
    Take the printed report with you to discuss your health history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Record Keeping

Regular exports create a backup system:

- Export monthly or quarterly for ongoing backups
- Store PDFs in a secure location
- Keep copies in cloud storage
- Maintain a chronological archive

<Tip>
  Set a recurring reminder to export your data regularly as part of your health management routine.
</Tip>

### Insurance Claims

Exported records can support insurance documentation:

- Proof of medical appointments
- History of health conditions
- Treatment compliance documentation
- Timeline of health events

## Privacy and Security

### Who Can Export

Only you can export your own health data:

- Users with shared access to your data **cannot** export your records
- Only the account owner has export permissions
- Each user can only export their own information

<Info>
  This ensures that your data remains under your control, even when shared with others.
</Info>

### Secure Your Exports

After downloading your PDF:

- Store it in a secure location
- Use password protection if your PDF viewer supports it
- Be cautious when sharing via email
- Delete copies from shared computers
- Keep sensitive medical information protected

## Technical Details

### File Format

Exports are generated as PDF documents:

- Universal format readable on any device
- Consistent appearance across platforms
- Suitable for printing
- Cannot be easily modified (maintaining data integrity)

### File Naming

Automatic naming convention:

```
historial-salud-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf
```

Example: `historial-salud-2026-03-05.pdf`

This naming helps you:
- Identify when each export was created
- Organize multiple exports chronologically
- Track which export is most recent

### Data Processing

The export process:

1. Retrieves all records from your account
2. Combines different record types
3. Sorts by date in descending order
4. Formats data for professional presentation
5. Generates PDF with your information
6. Triggers automatic download

<Note>
  The export only includes data up to the moment you generate it. New records added after export will not appear in that PDF.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

### Export Not Downloading

- Check your browser's download settings
- Ensure pop-ups are not blocked
- Try a different browser
- Check available disk space

### Empty or Incomplete Export

- Verify you have health records in your account
- Ensure all records have valid dates
- Contact your administrator if issues persist

### Large File Size

- Large histories may take longer to generate
- Be patient during the export process
- Consider exporting specific date ranges if available in future updates

<Tip>
  If you experience consistent export issues, try logging out and back in, then attempt the export again.
</Tip>

## Best Practices

### Regular Exports

- Create exports on a regular schedule (monthly, quarterly)
- Export before major medical appointments
- Generate a backup after adding significant new data
- Keep multiple versions for historical reference

### Organization

- Create a dedicated folder for health exports
- Use a consistent naming convention if renaming files
- Organize by date or medical event
- Consider cloud backup for important exports

### Verification

- Always open and review the PDF after export
- Verify that all expected data is present
- Check that dates and values are correct
- Confirm the export date is current

## Future Data Access

<Info>
  Exported PDFs are static snapshots of your data. They do not update automatically with new information.
</Info>

To maintain current records:

- Generate new exports when significant data is added
- Replace old exports with updated versions
- Keep multiple dated exports for historical comparison
- Consider the export date when sharing with healthcare providers
